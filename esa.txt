ENTITY TYPE: SPACE AGENCY

Organization: ESA
Full Name: European Space Agency
Region: Europe
Established: May 31, 1975
Current Member States: 23
Agency Type: Intergovernmental space organization
Headquarters: Paris, France

OVERVIEW:
The European Space Agency is an intergovernmental organization dedicated to the peaceful exploration and use of space.

FOUNDING:
ESA was established in 1975.

ESA brings European countries together to develop and operate space programs that would be difficult for individual countries to accomplish independently.

AREAS OF ACTIVITY:
ESA works in:

- Space science
- Earth observation
- Human spaceflight
- Telecommunications
- Navigation
- Launch systems
- Planetary exploration
- Astronomy
- Space technology

MAJOR PROGRAMS AND MISSIONS:
Examples include:

- Ariane launch systems
- Rosetta
- Gaia
- Mars Express
- Copernicus
- Euclid
- Solar Orbiter

INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION:
ESA works closely with NASA, JAXA, CSA and other international organizations.

ESA is a major partner in the James Webb Space Telescope mission.

ESA also contributes to the Artemis lunar exploration program.

FACILITIES:
ESA operates facilities and centers across Europe, including research, astronaut training, Earth observation, space operations and technology centers.

KEY TERMS:
ESA, European Space Agency, Europe, Ariane, Rosetta, Gaia, Mars Express, Copernicus, Webb.
