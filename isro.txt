ENTITY TYPE: SPACE AGENCY

Organization: ISRO
Full Name: Indian Space Research Organisation
Country: India
Established: August 15, 1969
Founder / Founding Figure: Dr. Vikram A. Sarabhai
Parent Department: Department of Space, Government of India
Agency Type: National space agency

OVERVIEW:
ISRO is India's national space agency responsible for developing and operating space technology, launch vehicles, satellites, and scientific missions.

FOUNDING:
India's organized space activities began with the Indian National Committee for Space Research (INCOSPAR) in 1962.

ISRO was formally established in 1969.

Dr. Vikram A. Sarabhai is widely recognized by ISRO as the founder of the Indian space programme.

ORGANIZATIONAL STRUCTURE:
ISRO operates under India's Department of Space.

The Space Commission and Department of Space were established in 1972, and ISRO was brought under the Department of Space.

MAJOR AREAS:
ISRO works in:

- Satellite communications
- Earth observation
- Navigation
- Meteorology
- Space science
- Planetary exploration
- Launch vehicle development
- Human spaceflight technology

LAUNCH VEHICLES:
Major launch vehicle families include:

- PSLV
- GSLV
- LVM3

MAJOR MISSIONS:
Important missions include:

- Aryabhata
- Chandrayaan-1
- Chandrayaan-2
- Chandrayaan-3
- Mars Orbiter Mission
- Aditya-L1

ACHIEVEMENTS:
ISRO has developed indigenous satellite and launch technologies and has conducted successful lunar and interplanetary missions.

Chandrayaan-3 demonstrated a successful lunar soft landing.

Mangalyaan demonstrated India's interplanetary mission capabilities.

KEY PERSON:
Dr. Vikram A. Sarabhai is regarded by ISRO as the founder of the Indian space programme.

KEY TERMS:
ISRO, India, Vikram Sarabhai, INCOSPAR, PSLV, GSLV, LVM3, Chandrayaan, Mangalyaan.
