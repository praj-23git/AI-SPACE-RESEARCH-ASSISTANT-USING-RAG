ENTITY TYPE: SPACE AGENCY

Organization: NASA
Full Name: National Aeronautics and Space Administration
Country: United States
Agency Type: Civilian space agency
Established: 1958
Official Operations Began: October 1, 1958
Predecessor: National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics (NACA)
First Administrator: T. Keith Glennan

OVERVIEW:
NASA is the United States government agency responsible for civilian space exploration, aeronautics research, and related scientific and technological activities.

FOUNDING:
The National Aeronautics and Space Act of 1958 established NASA.

The legislation was signed by President Dwight D. Eisenhower on July 29, 1958.

NASA officially began operations on October 1, 1958.

NASA was created partly from the existing National Advisory Committee for Aeronautics.

IMPORTANT PROGRAMS:
NASA has operated or led major programs including:

- Mercury
- Gemini
- Apollo
- Space Shuttle
- International Space Station
- Artemis

MAJOR AREAS:
NASA works in:

- Human spaceflight
- Planetary exploration
- Earth science
- Astrophysics
- Heliophysics
- Aeronautics
- Space technology

MAJOR MISSIONS:
Examples include:

- Apollo 11
- Mars Perseverance
- OSIRIS-REx
- James Webb Space Telescope
- Voyager
- Artemis

INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION:
NASA works with international space agencies and commercial aerospace companies on scientific missions, human spaceflight, and space exploration.

KEY PEOPLE:
Dwight D. Eisenhower signed the legislation creating NASA.

T. Keith Glennan was NASA's first administrator.

KEY TERMS:
NASA, United States, National Aeronautics and Space Administration, NACA, Apollo, Artemis, space exploration.
