ENTITY TYPE: SPACE AGENCY

Organization: CNSA
Full Name: China National Space Administration
Country: China
Agency Type: Government space organization

OVERVIEW:
The China National Space Administration is China's governmental organization responsible for civilian satellite management and intergovernmental space cooperation.

AREAS OF ACTIVITY:
China's space program includes:

- Earth observation
- Communications satellites
- Navigation
- Launch vehicles
- Human spaceflight
- Lunar exploration
- Planetary exploration
- Space science

MAJOR PROGRAMS:
Important Chinese space programs include:

- Chang'e lunar exploration program
- Tianwen planetary exploration program
- Tiangong space station
- BeiDou navigation system

LUNAR EXPLORATION:
The Chang'e program has conducted multiple lunar missions.

Chang'e-4 achieved the first soft landing on the far side of the Moon.

MARS EXPLORATION:
The Tianwen-1 mission successfully placed a spacecraft into Mars orbit and delivered the Zhurong rover to the Martian surface.

HUMAN SPACEFLIGHT:
China operates the Tiangong space station and conducts crewed spaceflight missions.

INTERNATIONAL COOPERATION:
CNSA participates in international and intergovernmental space cooperation.

KEY TERMS:
CNSA, China, Chang'e, Tianwen, Zhurong, Tiangong, BeiDou, lunar exploration, Mars.
