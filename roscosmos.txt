ENTITY TYPE: SPACE AGENCY

Organization: Roscosmos
Full Name: State Space Corporation Roscosmos
Country: Russia
Established as State Corporation: 2015
Agency Type: Russian state space corporation

OVERVIEW:
Roscosmos is Russia's state space corporation responsible for major areas of Russia's civilian space activities.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
Roscosmos is involved in:

- Launch vehicles
- Spacecraft
- Satellites
- Human spaceflight
- Space science
- Earth observation
- Space infrastructure

HISTORICAL BACKGROUND:
Russia's space program has roots in the Soviet space program.

Major historical achievements associated with Soviet and Russian spaceflight include:

- Sputnik 1
- Vostok 1
- Soyuz spacecraft
- Soviet lunar and planetary missions

HUMAN SPACEFLIGHT:
Roscosmos has played a major role in human spaceflight and International Space Station operations.

SOYUZ:
Soyuz spacecraft and launch vehicles have been central to Russian human spaceflight for decades.

SPACE EXPLORATION:
Russian and Soviet missions have explored the Moon, Venus, Mars and other Solar System targets.

KEY TERMS:
Roscosmos, Russia, Soviet space program, Soyuz, Sputnik, Vostok, ISS, human spaceflight.
